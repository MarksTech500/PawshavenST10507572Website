About Paws Haven

Learn more about our purpose and commitment to animal welfare.

Who We Are

Paws Haven Animal Rescue is a non-profit organisation concept created to support vulnerable, abandoned and neglected animals. The organisation aims to provide animals with care while working towards responsible and suitable adoptions.


This website is designed to make information easier to access and to give members of the public clear ways to adopt, volunteer, sponsor and contact the organisation.

Our Mission

To help animals in need by promoting rescue, rehabilitation, responsible adoption and community participation.

Our Values

The principles that guide the organisation.

🐶

Compassion

Every animal should be treated with care, kindness and respect.

🛡️

Responsibility

We encourage responsible ownership and informed adoption decisions.

🌱

Community

Working with the community can create better outcomes for animals.
