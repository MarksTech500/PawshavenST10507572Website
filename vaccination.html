The Pawshaven Adoption Health Promise

Every animal adopted through Pawshaven receives an eligible first vaccination at no additional charge as part of our adoption programme. We also encourage adopters to return annually for recommended vaccinations at an affordable, subsidised contribution.

PROTECTING OUR ANIMALS

Why Are Vaccinations Important?

Vaccinations play an important role in protecting pets against preventable infectious diseases.

  • Help protect against serious diseases.
  • Support long-term animal health.
  • Help protect other animals in the community.
  • Encourage responsible pet ownership.
